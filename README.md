# Humanize Me

**Humanize Me helps AI understand the person before it rewrites the words.**

It takes the user's natural communication—whether it is polished, fragmented, dictated, misspelled, nonlinear, or emotionally loaded—translates it into a clear understanding of what they actually mean, and then translates that meaning back into language that remains recognizably theirs. The result may be cleaner, stronger, or easier for others to understand, but it should never polish away the person behind it.

Created by **Grant Daniels** for people who want AI to understand their voice—not replace it.

## What makes it different

Most “humanizer” tools begin with AI-generated text and try to make it sound less artificial. Humanize Me begins with the human—and works one step earlier, where meaning is most likely to be lost.

It helps an agent:

- read through typos, shorthand, fragments, dictation errors, and nonlinear thinking
- identify the user's actual point, emotional position, audience, and desired outcome
- separate what the user means from the literal imperfections in how it first came out
- express that meaning more clearly while keeping the user's identity and perspective intact
- adapt communication for a wider audience without turning it into generic corporate copy

## Humanize Me vs. AI text humanizers

| | Humanize Me | Traditional AI humanizer |
|---|---|---|
| Starts with | A person's thoughts, speech, notes, or rough draft | Text already generated by AI |
| Primary job | Understand intent and translate it clearly | Remove robotic writing patterns |
| Protects | Meaning, identity, emotion, context, and audience fit | Natural-sounding style |
| Best trigger | “Help me explain what I mean” or “say this better” | “Make this AI draft sound less robotic” |
| Works at | The communication and intent layer | The wording and style layer |

Humanize Me is stronger when the challenge is not simply how the words sound, but whether AI understood the person correctly in the first place. If an AI draft already captures the right meaning and only needs stylistic cleanup, a traditional humanizer is the better tool. The two skills should complement each other, not compete.

## Use it

Copy this repository's skill files into a `humanize-me` skill folder in your agent's skills directory, or import the repository using your agent platform's supported skill workflow.

Example prompts:

```text
Use $humanize-me to understand what I'm trying to say and rewrite it clearly without losing my voice.
```

```text
Use $humanize-me to turn these rough thoughts into something my audience will understand.
```

```text
Use $humanize-me to write this from my perspective, but make it accessible to people who don't already know me.
```

The skill can also activate automatically for requests such as “say this better,” “help me explain what I mean,” “make this sound like me,” or “clean this up without polishing me out of it.”

## Philosophy

Human communication is more than correct grammar or polished sentences. People communicate through context, rhythm, emotion, shorthand, history, and the things they assume others already understand. Humanize Me teaches AI to interpret those signals before it decides how the final message should sound.
